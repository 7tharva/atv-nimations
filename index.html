Short animated "what if" stories

ATV-nimations is a YouTube channel that publishes original short animated stories with blocky 3D characters, each built around one strange rule, such as "your height depends on how many people believe in you".

Shorts Uploader

Shorts Uploader is a private command-line tool that the ATV-nimations channel owner runs on their own computer. It uploads the channel's own finished videos, with their titles and descriptions, to the ATV-nimations YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

  • It is used only by the channel owner, for the owner's own channel.
  • It does not access, collect or store anyone else's data.
  • It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
